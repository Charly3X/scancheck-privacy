# ScanCheck — privacy policy

The published privacy policy for the ScanCheck Android app, in English and
Russian. This repository exists only to serve that document at a stable public
address, as Google Play requires: a privacy policy link has to be both in Play
Console and inside the app.

- English: https://charly3x.github.io/scancheck-privacy/
- Русский: https://charly3x.github.io/scancheck-privacy/ru.html

The source of truth is `docs/privacy-policy.md` (Russian) and
`docs/privacy-policy.en.md` (English) in the app's own repository; the pages here
are generated from them. Change the policy there, regenerate, and push.
